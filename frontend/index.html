What the model reads

Every risk score is built from three streams of evidence, cross-checked against each other before an alert goes out.

Satellite imagery

Optical and radar passes track slope cover, moisture, and ground movement between visits — including through cloud cover.

Rainfall & terrain

Gridded rainfall history, slope angle, and soil type give the model the physical context behind every satellite frame.

Field reports

Local officers confirm what the model flags, and their notes feed back in to keep the risk scores grounded in reality.

From imagery to a warning

The same pipeline runs on every new satellite pass, so risk scores stay current without anyone requesting an update.

01 — Capture

New satellite pass

Fresh imagery and rainfall data are pulled in as soon as they're available.

02 — Process

Feature extraction

Terrain, vegetation, and moisture signals are computed for every monitored slope.

03 — Predict

Risk scoring

The trained model outputs a probability of failure for each zone.

04 — Alert

Notify the ground

Scores above threshold reach district teams within minutes.
